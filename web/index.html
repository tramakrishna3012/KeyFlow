Instant Fuzzy Search & Hotkey Snippet Recall

KeyFlow records your permitted keystrokes and text inputs continuously in the background. Hit your custom hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+K / Cmd+Shift+K) to search past typed snippets in under 200ms and reinsert them into any active application.

  • ✓ Application Categorization: Automatically tags snippets by IDE, browser, or chat client.

PRD §5.5 & TRD §4

Hardware-Backed Local Encryption

All recorded keystrokes and snippet history are stored in a local SQLite database protected by SQLCipher and AES-256-GCM encryption. Cryptographic master keys are kept in platform-native secure enclaves (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, Android Keystore).

  • ✓ Sensitive Redaction: Passwords, credit cards, and OTPs are purged before storage.
  • ✓ Configurable Exclusions: Blacklist password managers, banking apps, and private chats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about KeyFlow's privacy, capture scope, and security model.

KeyFlow captures permitted text entries and active application titles while a monitoring session is running. It indexes this data so you can search and reinsert your typing history.
KeyFlow includes built-in real-time regex sanitizers that detect and exclude credit/debit card numbers, 4–8 digit OTPs, passwords, authentication tokens, and banking details before any record is stored or synced.
By default, 100% of data is stored locally in an encrypted SQLCipher vault on your device. When optional cross-device cloud synchronization is enabled, records are transmitted encrypted with AES-256-GCM directly to your private backend instance.
On iOS, sandbox security policies prohibit background global keystroke monitoring. KeyFlow operates on iOS via a custom Keyboard Extension and widget that allows instant snippet search and insertion inside apps.
You can trigger an immediate one-click cryptographic wipe at any time from the Privacy & Exclusions tab in the web dashboard or inside the application settings.

About the Project

High Performance. Uncompromising Privacy.

KeyFlow was created to eliminate the friction of repetitive typing while keeping personal data strictly in the owner's hands.

KeyFlow combines native background daemons (written in Flutter, Rust/Kotlin, and Swift) with lightweight cryptographic storage. Operating locally with less than 50MB memory and <1% CPU overhead, it ensures seamless indexing without impacting device performance or battery life.

The project follows strict privacy-by-design standards: transparent status indicators, no hidden capture mechanisms, and complete cryptographic wipe capabilities that adhere to GDPR and CCPA Data Subject Rights (DSAR).
