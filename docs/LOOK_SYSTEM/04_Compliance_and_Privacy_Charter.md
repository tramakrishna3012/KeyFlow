# Look System Application: Compliance & Employee Privacy Charter

## 1. Transparency Commitment

The **Look System Application** is designed with the principle that workplace productivity analytics must never compromise personal dignity, privacy, or legal compliance (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, HIPAA, ECPA).

---

## 2. Privacy Guarantees

1. **No Keystroke Logging**: The application has no keystroke interception mechanisms, keylogging hooks, or text buffer recorders.
2. **No Screen Snooping**: Does not record desktop screens, take random screenshots of private conversations, or access webcams.
3. **No Clipboard Capture**: Clipboard copy/paste buffers are completely untouched.
4. **Data Minimization**: Only the foreground application executable name, coarse category, duration, and idle state are stored.
5. **Immediate User Controls**: Any authorized user can pause monitoring (for 15 minutes, 1 hour, or the remainder of the day) directly from their system tray or in-app interface.

---

## 3. GDPR & CCPA Compliance Mapping

* **Article 5 (Data Minimization & Storage Limitation)**: Strict policy-based retention window (default 90 days) with automatic purging.
* **Article 6 (Lawfulness & Consent)**: Explicit consent dialog with version-tracked consent records in the database.
* **Article 15 (Right of Access / DSAR)**: Self-service JSON/CSV telemetry download available directly in the user dashboard.
* **Article 17 (Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten)**: One-click personal data wipe permanently purging all historical activity logs.
* **Article 30 (Records of Processing Activities)**: Immutable audit log tracking all administrative, ingestion, and deletion events.
